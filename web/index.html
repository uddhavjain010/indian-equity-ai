System Architecture

End-to-End Quantitative Machine Learning

Designed specifically for Indian equities, bridging the gap between raw exchange data feeds, rigorous statistical preprocessing, deep learning, and rule-based trade signal intelligence.

Real-Time Indian Market Data

Seamlessly integrates NSE and BSE listed equities through dual providers: robust daily historical feeds via Yahoo Finance and low-latency tick streams via Upstox V3 Protobuf WebSocket feeds.

  • Comprehensive Nifty 50 ticker universe
  • Data validation against anomalies & splits
  • Pluggable abstraction provider architecture

Dual Machine Learning Core

Leverages the distinct complementary strengths of Deep Recurrent Networks (LSTM) for chronological time dependencies and Gradient Boosted Trees (XGBoost) for complex technical indicators.

  • 60-day rolling sequence lookback windows
  • Gradient boosting over non-linear feature maps
  • Weighted ensemble next-day target projection

Leak-Free Walk-Forward Backtesting

Eliminates the dangerous lookahead biases common in naive backtests. Uses expanding-window chronological splits where all feature scalers are fitted exclusively on preceding historical data.

  • Strict out-of-sample forward evaluation
  • No data shuffling or forward leakage
  • Academic evaluation metrics: RMSE, MAE, MAPE

Confluence Signal Engine

Synthesizes quantitative price projections with classical technical analysis rules to issue robust BUY, HOLD, or SELL research signals with clear explainability and confidence scoring.

  • RSI momentum and MACD trend verification
  • Ensemble agreement thresholds
  • Transparent educational research insights

Deep Learning & Gradient Boosting

Dual Machine Learning Architecture

Combining the sequential memory of Recurrent Neural Networks with the gradient-boosted decision surfaces of tree ensembles.

Deep Neural Network

Stacked LSTM Network

Optimized for Long-Range Sequential Autocorrelation

Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cells solve the vanishing gradient limitation of standard RNNs through specialized input, forget, and output gates. The network processes a 60-day rolling sequence of normalized multivariate market states to project the continuous next-day closing price.

Input Shape: (batch, 60 timesteps, n_features)
Layer 1: 64 Units (return_sequences=True)
Dropout Regularization: 20% Dropout between layers
Layer 2: 32 Units (Dense bottleneck)
Optimizer & Loss: Adam (lr=0.001) with Huber/MSE
Gradient Boosted Trees

XGBoost Regressor

Optimized for Non-Linear Tabular Feature Interactions

Extreme Gradient Boosting builds an ensemble of shallow decision trees in a stage-wise manner, minimizing a regularized objective function. It excels at discovering non-linear threshold rules across momentum, volatility, and volume indicators without assuming linearity.

Feature Inputs: Technical Indicators + 5 Lagged Return terms
Max Tree Depth: 5 (prevents leaf overfitting)
Estimators & Rate: 200 Estimators, Learning Rate 0.05
Subsample / Colsample: 0.8 / 0.8 stochastic sampling
Regularization: L1 (alpha=0.1) & L2 (lambda=1.0)

Model Evaluation & Statistical Validation Metrics

Every model checkpoint is strictly evaluated on out-of-sample data using four quantitative metrics:

RMSE
Root Mean Squared Error
Penalizes large outlier forecast errors heavily, critical for portfolio downside management.
MAE
Mean Absolute Error
Measures the average magnitude of absolute rupee price discrepancies in Indian Rupees (INR).
MAPE
Mean Absolute % Error
Scale-independent relative error percentage, allowing comparisons across low and high nominal price stocks.
DIR ACC
Directional Accuracy (%)
Percentage of trading sessions where the predicted price movement direction (Up/Down) matched reality.
Methodology & Rigor

Walk-Forward Validation & Backtesting

Standard random train-test splitting introduces catastrophic lookahead bias in time series. Our platform utilizes strict expanding-window walk-forward validation.

Expanding Window Validation

At each fold, the model trains strictly on data up to time T and predicts out-of-sample point T+1. The window expands chronologically, simulating the exact real-world scenario of an algorithmic fund trading daily.

Zero Future-Data Leakage

Data normalization scalers (MinMaxScaler / StandardScaler) are fitted solely on the training portion of the dataset. Testing and validation rows are transformed using pre-fitted parameters without peeking into future distributions.

Academic Cost Disclaimer

In line with academic research standards, current walk-forward simulations focus on statistical price and directional prediction. Brokerage commissions, STT, exchange turnover fees, and execution slippage are deliberately excluded from this theoretical model.

Quantitative Inputs

Technical Indicators & Engineered Features

Over 25 causal quantitative indicators computed on open, high, low, close, and volume series without lookahead leakage.

Momentum

RSI (14-period)

Relative Strength Index measures the speed and change of price movements to identify overbought (>70) and oversold (<30) conditions.

Trend Following

MACD (12, 26, 9)

Moving Average Convergence Divergence tracks trend strength, momentum shifts, and signal line crossovers.

Moving Averages

EMA (9, 21, 50, 200)

Exponential Moving Averages provide dynamic support/resistance levels with heavier weighting on recent price bars.

Volatility

Bollinger Bands (20, 2σ)

Upper and lower standard deviation envelopes around a 20-day SMA quantify statistical price dispersion and squeeze breakouts.

Volume Flow

Volume SMA & OBV

On-Balance Volume and volume moving averages confirm price action by quantifying institutional accumulation vs distribution.

Time Series

Lagged Returns (t-1 to t-5)

Autoregressive percentage return lags across the previous 5 trading days capture short-term mean-reversion and momentum shocks.

Price Action

OHLC Volatility Ratios

Intraday range (High - Low) / Close and overnight gap (Open - Prev Close) capture intraday expansion dynamics.

Target Variable

Next-Day Close (t+1)

The primary prediction target: tomorrow's closing price and binary direction, strictly shifted by -1 row during dataset generation.
